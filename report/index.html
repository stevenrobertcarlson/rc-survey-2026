Releasing Community Survey Results · August 2026

41 people paused for five minutes to tell us who we are

The forty-one people who answered this survey represent the most engaged members of the Releasing Community. Coaches play an active role. A strong majority of us are trained facilitators. Three findings in particular will shape how this community develops from here.

what this changes

Three things I am taking from this

  • 01
    We are broader than one lineageKate Freeman and Larry Crane practitioners are here, alongside Hale Dwoskin's students, who still constitute the majority. I created the Open-Mic Support Calls group as an experiment, open to anyone who wants to host, certified or not, provided they work with releasing modalities. This group has proven popular with the community.
  • 02
    The Method works as a toolkit alongside other modalitiesNearly half of us use the Sedona Method in combination with other healing modalities. It serves many of us as a toolbox that complements other work. This is a strength worth naming out loud.
  • 03
    Asia Pacific is growingOur membership is roughly split between Europe (46%) and North America (39%), and so most of our call times are optimized for those time zones. Asia Pacific is now 15%. We recently tried to schedule a practice call for Thursday mornings in Asia, but attendance was thin. We will continue to look for ways to connect with these releasers.

Thank you to everyone who took the time to answer. The community is at releasing.community.
