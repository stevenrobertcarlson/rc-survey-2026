Three things I am taking from this

01

We are broader than one lineage

Kate Freeman and Larry Crane practitioners are here, alongside Hale Dwoskin's students, who still constitute the majority. I created the Open-Mic Support Calls group as an experiment, open to anyone who wants to host, certified or not, provided they work with releasing modalities. This group has proven popular with the community.

02

The Method works as a toolkit alongside other modalities

Nearly half of us use the Sedona Method in combination with other healing modalities. It serves many of us as a toolbox that complements other work. This is a strength worth naming out loud.

03

Asia-Pacific is growing

We currently have a practice call scheduled for Wednesdays at 5pm Pacific, which lands Thursday morning in Indonesia and Sydney. We will continue to work to schedule call times that are convenient to Asia Pacific.

Thank you to everyone who took the time to answer.
